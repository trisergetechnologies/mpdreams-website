PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy applies to the website and mobile application of Dream Mart, owned and operated by AARUSH MP DREAMS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). It explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you browse, register, place an order, or contact us.

When do we collect information?

We collect information when you create a Dream Mart account, place or track an order, add a delivery address, make a payment, contact support, fill a form on our website, or otherwise use the Dream Mart app or site.

What information we collect

- Identity and contact details: name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.

- Account details: login credentials and order history.

- Transaction details: products purchased, amounts paid, and payment status. Payment card or UPI data is processed by authorised payment partners.

- Device and usage information needed to operate the app or website (for example device type and app activity).

How do we use your information?

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and manage your Dream Mart account.

- Process, fulfil, ship, and support orders.

- Communicate about orders, refunds, deliveries, and customer support.

- Personalize product offerings and improve the shopping experience.

- Send service-related messages and, where permitted, promotional communication. You may opt out of marketing emails.

- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

Third Party Disclosure

We do not sell your personally identifiable information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate Dream Mart, including payment gateways, logistics partners (such as India Post), and IT or communication providers, only as needed to fulfil your order or run the service, and as required by law.

Third Party Links

Dream Mart may contain links to app stores, payment pages, or courier tracking sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for content or policies on third-party sites.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information. We retain data only as long as needed for the purposes above, order records, and legal obligations.

Contacting Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data on Dream Mart, contact AARUSH MP DREAMS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED at info@aarushmp.com or ampdreammart@gmail.com.
